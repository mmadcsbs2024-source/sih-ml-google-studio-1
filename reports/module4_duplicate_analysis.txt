========================================
PHYSICAL LOCATION vs HISTORICAL OCCURRENCE
========================================
1. PHYSICAL LANDSLIDE LOCATION
   A persistent landslide location represented by spatial evidence such as validated coordinates, state/district consistency, and slide-name similarity.
2. HISTORICAL OCCURRENCE
   A documented event or reactivation at that location, represented through History text and event date/date-range fields.
3. IMPORTANT: a single physical location may have multiple historical occurrences, e.g. '2016, reactivated on 14 May 2022'.
4. Therefore, Slide No alone is not a reliable physical-event identifier.
